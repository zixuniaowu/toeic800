  1. 1
    budget复习
    /ˈbʌdʒɪt/n./v.
    预算；编预算
    The marketing department has a budget of fifty thousand dollars for the trade show.
    市场部为这次展会准备了五万美元的预算。
  2. 2
    expenditure重点复习
    /ɪkˈspɛndətʃɚ/n.
    支出；开支
    The company plans to reduce its expenditure on office supplies next year.
    公司计划明年减少办公用品方面的支出。
  3. 3
    revenue复习
    /ˈrɛvəˌnu/n.
    收入；营业额
    Revenue from online sales increased by fifteen percent this quarter.
    本季度线上销售收入增长了百分之十五。
  4. 4
    profit
    /ˈprɑfət/n.
    利润
    The new product line helped the company make a record profit.
    新产品线帮助公司创下了利润纪录。
  5. 5
    deficit复习
    /ˈdɛfəsət/n.
    赤字；亏损
    The branch reported a small deficit because of high rent costs.
    由于房租成本高，这家分店报告了少量亏损。
  6. 6
    audit复习
    /ˈɔdɪt/n./v.
    审计；查账
    An outside firm will audit our accounts in November.
    一家外部公司将在十一月审计我们的账目。
  7. 7
    accountant
    /əˈkaʊntənt/n.
    会计师；会计
    Please send all receipts to the accountant by Friday.
    请在周五前把所有收据交给会计。
  8. 8
    fiscal year重点复习
    /ˈfɪskəl jɪr/n.
    财政年度；会计年度
    Our fiscal year begins on April first.
    我们的财政年度从四月一日开始。
  9. 9
    quarterly
    /ˈkwɔrtɚli/adj./adv.
    季度的；每季度
    The quarterly report will be presented at the board meeting.
    季度报告将在董事会上汇报。
  10. 10
    forecast
    /ˈfɔrˌkæst/n./v.
    预测；预报
    Analysts forecast strong sales during the holiday season.
    分析师预测节假日期间销售会很强劲。
  11. 11
    transaction
    /trænˈzækʃən/n.
    交易；（一笔）业务
    Every transaction is recorded in the online system.
    每一笔交易都记录在在线系统里。
  12. 12
    deposit
    /dəˈpɑzɪt/n./v.
    存款；押金；存入
    A deposit of twenty percent is required to confirm the order.
    确认订单需要支付百分之二十的定金。
  13. 13
    withdraw
    /wɪðˈdrɔ/v.
    取款；撤回
    Customers can withdraw cash from any of our machines free of charge.
    顾客可以在我们任何一台机器上免费取现金。
  14. 14
    loan
    /loʊn/n.
    贷款；借款
    The bakery received a loan to open a second location.
    这家面包店获得了一笔贷款，用来开第二家店。
  15. 15
    interest rate
    /ˈɪntrəst reɪt/n.
    利率
    The bank lowered its interest rate for small businesses.
    银行降低了面向小企业的利率。
  16. 16
    investment
    /ˌɪnˈvɛstmənt/n.
    投资
    The new software was a good investment for our team.
    这款新软件对我们团队来说是一项很好的投资。
  17. 17
    shareholder
    /ˈʃɛrˌhoʊldɚ/n.
    股东
    Shareholders will vote on the proposal at the annual meeting.
    股东们将在年会上对这项提案进行投票。
  18. 18
    financial statement复习
    /fəˈnænʃəl ˈsteɪtmənt/n.
    财务报表
    The financial statement shows that costs rose last year.
    财务报表显示去年成本上升了。
  19. 19
    tax
    /tæks/n.
    税；税款
    All prices on the menu include tax.
    菜单上的所有价格都已含税。
  20. 20
    deduct
    /dɪˈdʌkt/v.
    扣除；减去
    The fee will be deducted from your monthly salary.
    这笔费用将从你的月薪中扣除。
  21. 21
    receipt
    /rɪˈsit/n.
    收据；收到
    Keep your receipt in case you need to return the item.
    请保留收据，以备需要退货。
  22. 22
    cost-effective重点复习
    /kɑst-ɪˈfɛktɪv/adj.
    划算的；性价比高的
    Video conferencing is a cost-effective way to hold meetings.
    视频会议是一种开会的经济划算的方式。
  23. 23
    spreadsheet
    /ˈsprɛdˌʃit/n.
    电子表格
    I've entered all the travel costs into a spreadsheet.
    我已经把所有差旅费用输入到电子表格里了。
  24. 24
    approval
    /əˈpruvəl/n.
    批准；认可
    Any purchase over five hundred dollars needs the manager's approval.
    任何超过五百美元的采购都需要经理批准。
  25. 25
    cut costs
    /kʌt kɑsts/phr.
    削减成本
    We need to cut costs without lowering the quality of our service.
    我们需要在不降低服务质量的前提下削减成本。
  26. 26
    balance
    /ˈbæləns/n.
    余额；结余；平衡
    You can check your account balance on our mobile app.
    您可以在我们的手机应用上查询账户余额。
  27. 27
    payroll
    /ˈpeɪˌroʊl/n.
    工资单；工资发放
    The payroll department pays all staff on the twenty-fifth of each month.
    工资部门在每月二十五日给全体员工发工资。
  28. 28
    accurate
    /ˈækjɚət/adj.
    准确的；精确的
    Please make sure the figures in your report are accurate.
    请确保你报告中的数字是准确的。
  29. 29
    discrepancy重点复习
    /dɪˈskrɛpənsi/n.

  30. 30
    projected重点复习
    /prəˈdʒɛktəd/adj.

    The projected cost of the new warehouse is two million dollars.
    新仓库的预计成本是两百万美元。
