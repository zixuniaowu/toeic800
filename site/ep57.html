    We will mark the anniversary with a party.

    naval复习

    /θru/prep./adv.
    直到……（包括在内）；穿过；通过
    The office is open Monday through Friday.
    办公室周一到周五营业。

    between重点复习
    /bɪˈtwin/prep.
    在……之间
    You can move money between accounts for free.
    你可以免费在账户之间转账。

    charge复习
    /tʃɑrdʒ/v./n.

    The bank does not charge fees for transfers.

    The floor polisher is in the closet.

    The maintenance closet is next to the elevator.

    Monroe County is in the north of the state.
    Monroe 县在州的北部。

    Each time you shop, we donate to charity.
    你每次购物，我们都会向慈善机构捐款。

    portion复习
    /ˈpɔrʃən/n.

    A portion of each sale goes to charity.
    每笔销售的一部分会捐给慈善机构。

    transaction复习
    /trænˈzækʃən/n.

    Each transaction is recorded on your card.
    每笔交易都会记录在你的卡上。
  22. 22
    charity复习
    /ˈtʃɛrɪti/n.

    You can choose the charity you want to support.

    投资者期待良好的投资回报。
